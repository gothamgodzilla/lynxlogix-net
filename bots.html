Systematic workflow · roles, not unsupervised spenders

Ten bots.
One human key.

Sophistication is division of labor. All ten may speak. None may route an order, sign a wallet, or hold a seed phrase.

01 Ingestor

Catch the bell

Normalizes TradingView, Hopper, and watchlist events into one ticket schema. Drops malformed noise.

02 Regime

Name the weather

Trend, range, or chaos. If chaos, size collapses toward zero before you see the ticket.

03 Risk veto

The cold no

Crowded funding, gap risk, correlation to open paper book. A veto is a first-class outcome.

04 Sizer

Percent of equity

0.25–1.00% until thirty paper days exist. Never “all in.” Never martingale language.

05 Scribe

One-page brief

Pair, side, why-now, why-not, invalidation, venue. Written so a tired human can decide in ninety seconds.

06 Devil

Argue the other side

Required paragraph. If the devil cannot write three sentences, the ticket is fashion, not a trade.

07 Compliance tone

Keep the house legal

Strips return promises, “guaranteed,” and advice-to-the-public voice from any outward brief.

08 Liaison

Sell Mangasm, not coins

Never trades. Routes UHNW attention to Mangasm+ and the house pass. This is the sales bot.

09 Night watch

Alerts only

Wakes you for invalidation or daily-loss stop. Does not “just take the fill” at 3 a.m.

10 Journal

After the key

Records approve / reject / hold and the would-have fill. The book is how 10x process is measured.
