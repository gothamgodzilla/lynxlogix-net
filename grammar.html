TradingView and Hopper may fire automatically. The webhook only writes a ticket. executed stays false. You still type the phrase and press the key.

Turnkey alert body

JSON the hook already accepts

{{
  "source": "tradingview",
  "pair": "BTC-USD",
  "side": "buy",
  "reason": "break of range high with volume",
  "invalidation": "close back inside range",
  "size": "0.50% equity paper",
  "venue": "coinbase-paper",
  "hopper": false
}}

POST /api/hook?key=paper — or your LYNX_HOOK_KEY. Response always includes executed: false.

Paste into a TradingView alert webhook body. Do not attach exchange API secrets.

Human gate after the bell

What happens next

1. Hook normalizes the payload into a ticket.

2. Brief Factory or a Grok scribe writes why-now / why-not.

3. You type PAPER ONLY, then Approve / Reject / Hold.

4. Ledger records a would-have. Coinbase, Kraken, and Phantom stay untouched.
